Visualizer Interactivity Features

The visualizer offers various interactive features for exploring data, courtesy of the underlying Plotly.js library. These are briefly presented below with examples.

1. Download plot as PNG

Download the current plot as a PNG file — useful for saving or sharing results.

2. Zoom (select region of interest)

Click and drag over a region to zoom in. Double-click to reset to the original view.

3. Pan

Click and drag to pan the plot in any direction. Double-click to reset.

4 & 5. Zoom in / Zoom out

Use the toolbar buttons or the mouse scroll wheel to zoom. Double-click to reset.

6. Autoscale

Resets the plot to fit all data. Useful after zooming or panning.

7. Reset axes

Resets only the axes of the plot, similar to Autoscale but without rescaling the data range.

8. Toggle Spike Lines

Toggles spike lines (the vertical and horizontal crosshairs on hover) on and off.

9 & 10. Show closest / Compare data on hover

Hover over the plot to display the closest data point values, or compare multiple traces at once. Especially useful when multiple signals are shown.
